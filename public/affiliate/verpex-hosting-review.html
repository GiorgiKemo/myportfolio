Who Verpex fits, and who should skip it

Good fit

  • WordPress blogs, portfolios and brochure sites
  • Small businesses that want email on their own domain
  • Freelancers hosting several client sites (Silver)
  • Anyone moving from a host they've outgrown, who wants the migration done for them

Probably skip

  • Next.js, Node or React apps deployed from Git: a platform like Vercel or a VPS fits better
  • Sites that must be billed monthly and want the free domain
  • High-traffic stores that need dedicated resources from day one

My take as a developer

What I look at in any host is recovery, renewal cost and exit. Verpex does well on the first: daily off-site backups and free migrations are included, not sold as add-ons. On renewal cost, the published renewal prices ($6, $10 and $15 a month) are moderate for cPanel hosting, and the calculator above shows the real total. On exit, cPanel is the industry-standard panel, so moving away later is a well-trodden path.

The trade-off is the hosting model. Shared cPanel hosting is great for PHP and WordPress. It's the wrong tool for apps built with modern JavaScript frameworks and CI pipelines, which is most of what I build for clients.

How to get started

  1. Pick a plan with the calculator above. Leave headroom on storage.
  2. Choose yearly billing if you want the free domain; monthly billing excludes it.
  3. Ask support to migrate your existing site. It's free.
  4. Test a restore from a daily backup in your first week, so you know it works before you need it.

Verpex FAQ

How much does Verpex cost after the first term?
Verpex lists renewal prices of $6/month (Bronze), $10/month (Silver) and $15/month (Gold). First-term prices are often lower. Check the current offer on Verpex.
Does Verpex include a free domain?
Yes on non-monthly billing, for many common extensions such as .com, .net, .org, .uk and .in. Premium extensions are excluded.
Is Verpex good for WordPress?
Yes. Every plan includes cPanel, one-click WordPress installs, daily backups and free SSL, and Verpex migrates existing sites for free.
Does Verpex have a money-back guarantee?
Verpex advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee. License add-on purchases are final and non-refundable.
Which plan should I choose?
Bronze for one site, Silver for up to 100 sites, Gold for unlimited sites. Choose the smallest plan that covers your site count and storage with headroom.
