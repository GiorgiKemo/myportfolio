Builder comparison guide

Choose the builder by what you must keep.

An AI-generated first draft is only the beginning. The durable decision is whether you can own the domain, prove the form works, control the content, and leave without losing the business.

Disclosure: this is an independent comparison checklist. The SiteMind link below is a paid referral link; it costs you nothing extra. Public commission claims describe partner offers, not guaranteed income; verify current product, affiliate, payout, and cancellation terms before subscribing.

1. Start with the job

Decide whether the first release is a landing page, portfolio, lead funnel, store, documentation site, or client handoff. A builder that is fast for one job may be a poor fit for another.

2. Test ownership and export

Confirm domain control, content export, code or data portability, account roles, backups, and the exact result of cancellation. “Built quickly” does not mean “easy to move.”

3. Prove the conversion path

Submit the form from a phone, confirm notifications, test spam protection, inspect the thank-you state, and verify analytics only after consent and the meaningful event are defined.

4. Inspect the generated output

Check headings, labels, keyboard focus, contrast, responsive layout, page speed, metadata, canonical URLs, and visible text. AI output still needs a human quality gate.

5. Price the whole year

Include the domain, renewal price, required plan tier, form or traffic limits, add-ons, transaction fees, seats, support, and the time needed to fix generated mistakes.

6. Separate a demo from a client system

Before deploying for a client, confirm privacy terms, data processors, access roles, source ownership, incident handling, and who is responsible for updates and support.
