AI receptionist or website assistant? Start with the channel.

The best choice depends less on the word “AI” and more on where customers already ask for help: the phone, your website, or both.

The short answer

Choose an AI receptionist when calls are the bottleneck.

It is the better starting point when missed calls, appointment requests, messages, and phone-based intake are costing you opportunities. Get Assistant describes an iPhone business line, an AI receptionist, appointment capture, transcripts, and call recaps.

Choose a website assistant when pages are the bottleneck.

It is the better starting point when visitors repeatedly ask about services, policies, pricing, or documentation. SiteMind describes a website-grounded assistant with source citations, scheduled recrawls, and a one-line widget install.

These are workflow distinctions, not a claim that either provider will produce a particular business result.

When a hybrid makes sense

A local service business can need both: the website assistant answers “Do you serve my area?” while the phone assistant captures a caller who wants a quote. The important part is to avoid two disconnected promises. Decide which system owns the first response, where the human takes over, and how the team sees the resulting lead.

Map the question to the channel.

Use the website for documented, searchable questions. Use the phone for urgency, nuance, and customers who prefer speaking.

Write the handoff before launch.

Define what “someone will follow up” means: who, when, through which system, and with what context.

Test unknowns, not just demos.

Ask a question that is not in the source material. A trustworthy assistant should make the boundary visible instead of inventing an answer.

Measure qualified outcomes.

Track answered questions, missed-call recovery, appointment requests, qualified leads, and human follow-up—not just chat volume.

A sensible starting path

If your website already contains the answers people need, start with a small SiteMind test and compare the assistant's citations and refusal behavior against real questions. If the pain is missed calls, review the Get Assistant product details instead. You do not need to adopt both systems to learn which channel is leaking demand.
