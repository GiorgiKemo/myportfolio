🛒 WordPress launch guide

Separate the store launch from the tool shopping.

A WordPress or WooCommerce project is a living system: content, hosting, checkout, updates, customer support, and recovery all matter. Use this checklist before committing to a stack.

Define the job before choosing a product

Clarify whether the project is a publication, service site, membership, catalog, or store. The right stack follows the operating model.

Separate content from commerce

List pages, products, payments, taxes, shipping, email, analytics, and support. Do not call a visual prototype a ready-to-sell store.

Plan the maintenance budget

Include hosting, domains, paid extensions, backups, security, transaction fees, support time, and the cost of testing updates.

Prove the checkout path

Run a test order in the real environment, verify confirmation emails and fulfillment, and document what happens when payment fails or an order is refunded.

Keep ownership portable

Use an account and domain you control, export customer and content data where permitted, and document admin access before launch.
