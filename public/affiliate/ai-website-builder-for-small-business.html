Small-business website guide

Choose the builder by the business you must keep.

An AI builder can shorten the first draft, but the right choice depends on what happens after launch: ownership, forms, SEO, integrations, support, and the cost of fixing a wrong decision.

Disclosure: this is an independent comparison guide. The provider program links below are informational references only; no paid affiliate link is active on this page. Confirm current product, affiliate, payout, renewal, and cancellation terms before subscribing.

Start with the operating model

PathBest whenWatch first
Self-serve builderYou have a simple offer, predictable pages, and time to maintain it.Renewal cost, export, forms, analytics, and support boundaries.
AI-assisted builderYou need a fast first draft and can review every important claim.Generated errors, locked-in content, SEO controls, and plan limits.
Developer-led buildYou need custom integrations, ownership, performance, or a durable handoff.Scope, maintenance, content ownership, and the cost of ongoing changes.

1. Write the one-sentence job

Define whether the site needs to generate inquiries, sell a product, book appointments, publish proof, or explain a service. A builder should serve that job, not become the job.

2. Test domain and content ownership

Confirm who controls the domain, account, copy, images, form data, analytics, and billing. Ask what can be exported after cancellation and what cannot.

3. Prove the conversion path

Submit every form on a phone, check notifications, test spam protection, inspect the thank-you state, and confirm that the inquiry reaches a real person.

4. Check SEO and accessibility

Inspect headings, labels, focus states, contrast, mobile layout, page speed, metadata, canonicals, redirects, sitemap controls, and indexability.

5. Price the whole year

Include the domain, renewal, required plan, traffic or form limits, seats, add-ons, transaction fees, support, and the time needed to correct generated content.

6. Price the exit

Write down the migration plan before subscribing. If the business cannot move its domain, content, leads, and measurement, the apparent speed may be expensive later.
