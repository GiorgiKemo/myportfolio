How to read your result

  • Conversations = visitors × chat rate. If you don't know your chat rate, start at 1–2% and adjust after the trial.
  • Break-even leads = monthly plan cost ÷ what one lead is worth to you. If it's 1–2 leads a month, the bar is low.
  • The trial is the real test. Three days of real traffic and the Content Gaps report tell you more than any calculator.

Good fit, or not yet?

Good fit

  • You answer the same questions by email every week
  • Your site has clear content (services, pricing, policies) to learn from
  • A single lead is worth tens or hundreds of dollars

Not yet

  • Under ~1,000 visitors a month: fix traffic first
  • Your site content is thin or out of date
  • You need phone answering rather than on-site chat
