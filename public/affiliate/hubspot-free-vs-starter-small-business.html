📇 Small-business buying guide

Choose the HubSpot plan your business can actually use.

A free CRM can be a sensible starting point. A paid plan can be useful when a real limit, permission need, or workflow gap costs more than the upgrade. Use this checklist before buying on a feature list alone.

Start with primary sources. Read HubSpot's current CRM overview, then compare the official pricing page for the exact hub, seats, billing period, and limits you need.

Name the customer job

Write down the first job the system must handle: contact records, a simple deal pipeline, lead capture, follow-up, support, or reporting. If the job is vague, a bigger plan will not fix the buying decision.

Separate free access from free capacity

Check the exact number of users, contacts, emails, forms, automations, reports, and credits included. “Free tools” describes an entry point; it does not mean every product feature or usage limit is free.

Count the people who need access

List the owner, sales users, support users, marketing users, and anyone who only needs reporting. Seat pricing and permission needs can change the real monthly cost quickly.

Test the handoff

Put one real inquiry through the workflow. Confirm who receives it, where it is stored, what reminders exist, and how a person can correct an inaccurate record.

Check migration and ownership

Before importing contacts, identify export options, field mapping, duplicate handling, account ownership, and the process for removing a user without losing business history.

Price the next twelve months

Include seats, contacts, credits, add-ons, onboarding, migration, implementation time, renewal terms, taxes, and the cost of changing plans later. Compare the total with the value of the job it replaces.

Define the upgrade trigger

Upgrade when a measured limit blocks a valuable workflow or the saved time and recovered opportunities justify the cost. Do not upgrade because a feature page makes the larger plan feel safer.

Decision rule: start with the smallest plan that handles one important customer job, document the first constraint you hit, and verify the paid plan removes that constraint before subscribing. Compare current tiers on HubSpot's official pricing page.
