Name the target job

Use one real job description when possible. A resume that tries to speak to every role usually becomes vague, repetitive, and harder to evaluate.

Separate facts from positioning

Confirm dates, titles, metrics, tools, and responsibilities first. Then improve ordering, wording, and emphasis without inventing achievements or credentials.

Check the actual deliverables

Ask whether you receive an editable Word file, a PDF, a targeted version, revision access, and a clear handoff. Do not confuse a visual preview with a usable application file.

Review ATS readability

Use conventional headings, selectable text, consistent dates, and a layout that survives parsing. ATS compatibility is a practical formatting goal, not a guarantee of ranking or employment.

Protect personal information

Share only what the service needs, understand who can access the documents, and check retention, deletion, payment, and refund terms before uploading sensitive career history.
