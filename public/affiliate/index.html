How does The Field Guide make money?
Some links are affiliate links. If you buy through one, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Every affiliate link is labelled next to where it appears.
Do affiliate links change the price I pay?
No. You pay the provider's normal price. Some partners, such as OneHomeschool, give a small discount when you use my code.
How do you choose which tools to cover?
I cover tools that matter to the websites and businesses I build for. Each guide lists who a tool fits, who it does not fit, and what to check on the official site before paying.
Can you set one of these tools up for me?
Yes. I'm a full-stack developer. Hosting migrations, AI assistant setup and website fixes are regular work for me. Email me with what you need.
