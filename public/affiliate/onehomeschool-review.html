Good fit, or not quite?

Likely a good fit

  • You already own your curriculum and want the daily plan done for you
  • You teach two or more children on different programs or paces
  • You want attendance and transcripts kept as you go
  • Your kids are ready to check off their own work

Maybe not

  • You want a tool that sells or chooses a curriculum for you
  • Your main programs aren't on the supported list yet
  • You need it to decide whether you meet your state's rules; that's between you and your state
