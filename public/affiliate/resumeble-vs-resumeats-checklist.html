Choose the scope before you choose the resume service.

A single target application and a full career repositioning are different jobs. Compare the scope, handoff, privacy, and revision process before paying for either.

Focused one-job concierge

Useful when you already know the target role and need a bounded review, tailoring pass, and practical ATS-readable files for one application.

Broader professional support

Useful when you need a new career narrative, a full resume, cover letter, LinkedIn profile, coaching, or support across several job-search materials.

Start with the deliverable

Confirm whether the package includes an editable document, PDF, target-job tailoring, cover letter, LinkedIn work, interview support, or only feedback. A polished preview is not the same as a reusable application file.

Match the price to the job

Do not buy a broad package when one application needs a focused cleanup. Conversely, do not expect a one-off edit to solve a career transition, weak evidence, or an unclear role direction.

Ask how facts are verified

Dates, titles, metrics, tools, and responsibilities should come from you. Strong positioning improves emphasis and clarity; it does not invent achievements, credentials, or employment history.

Check revisions and handoff

Before paying, ask how many revisions are included, who owns the files, whether the source stays editable, how feedback is exchanged, and what happens if the first draft misses the target.

Protect sensitive career data

Review upload, retention, deletion, payment, refund, and third-party access terms. Share only what the service needs and keep a private copy of every final document.

Treat outcome claims carefully

Interview or salary statistics may be based on a provider's own sample and method. They are not a promise about your application, employer, market, or timeline.
