Privacy notice

This notice covers the current Chvenori prelaunch information website.

What this website does

This website explains Chvenori and its initial plans. It does not create dating accounts, accept identity documents, or connect members.

If you contact us

If you send an email, your email address and message reach the founder’s mailbox. We use that information to reply to your question. Opening the email link does not send a message.

Hosting and technical data

The website is delivered through Vercel and GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request data to deliver and secure the site. Read the hosting providers’ privacy policies for more information. We do not add advertising or analytics cookies.

A future service

If the matchmaking service launches, its data handling, AI use, and deletion terms will be published before profiles are accepted. The current website does not establish that those features are available.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of a message you sent us, email contact@giorgi.codes.
