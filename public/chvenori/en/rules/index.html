Community rules

These rules describe the standards planned for Chvenori. The matchmaking service has not launched yet.

A shared purpose

Chvenori will be for adults aged 18 and over who sincerely seek a relationship with marriage in mind. Using the service for casual hookups, sexual services, advertising, or another purpose will be prohibited.

Honest information

Profile information must be truthful. Impersonation, fake profiles, and deliberately hiding relationship intentions or important facts will be prohibited. The verification process will be defined alongside the first-version details.

Consent and respect

Conversations and meetings require mutual consent. Respect a refusal. Harassment, threats, pressure, hate speech, and unwanted sexual content will be prohibited.

No scams

Fraud, demands for money, spam, and deliberate deception will be prohibited. Profiles will not be available for commercial solicitation.

Review and appeals

We plan blocking and reporting tools. Human moderators will review reports and account suspensions. Automated tools may assist that work; final decisions will remain under human review. The appeals process will be published before launch.

Your choice

An introduction creates no obligation to marry. Chvenori does not guarantee a match, marriage, or children. Each person makes their own decisions.
