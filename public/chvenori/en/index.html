Why Chvenori

Make your intention
clear from the start.

When you want marriage, it helps to meet someone who wants the same. Some people feel uncomfortable with dating apps because they associate them with casual relationships. We want to create a Georgian space where the purpose is clear from the first introduction.

Chvenori is at the prelaunch concept stage. We are preparing the first version and seeking feedback. Member registration is not open yet.

FAQ

Before we launch

Can I create a profile now?

Not yet. Chvenori is at the prelaunch stage. You can contact us with feedback or questions about the first version.

Will marriage be compulsory?

No. Membership will be for people seeking marriage, but every introduction and relationship decision will remain voluntary.

How will you use AI?

We plan to use Claude Code to build and test the product. We will also evaluate the Claude API for user-approved profile-writing support and assistance for human moderators reviewing reports. These features are not deployed yet.

What about privacy?

The planned first version includes private communication, controls over sharing, blocking, and reporting. This prelaunch website does not collect dating profiles.
